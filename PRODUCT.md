# Weiterwerk Bildungszentrum

A fictional education and vocational training centre portfolio template. Audience: adults considering a career change, practical upskilling or career coaching, and the education providers Khaled approaches. The page must demonstrate programme discovery, mode filtering, course content, funding orientation and a consultation enquiry. German and English. Programmes, durations and people are synthetic examples, clearly disclosed. No genuine qualifications, accreditation, partners, outcome statistics, testimonials or real contact destinations. The consultation form displays a browser-local preview and transmits nothing. A city map may show Kassel as an illustrative city, never an actual school location.

Preserve the existing portfolio family's Bricolage Grotesque / DM Sans, cobalt and navy palette, semantic HTML, light canvas, local assets and restrained reduced-motion-aware interactions. Adapt composition specifically for education/training centres rather than repeating a language school's offers. Static HTML/CSS/JS, no installation/build required, Portless for local preview. One new folder/repo/Vercel project. Campaign planning is a later task.
